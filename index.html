Mind’s Eye

A free app for measuring and training mental imagery, from the Imagination and Modal Cognition Lab at Duke University.

Mind’s Eye measures how vividly you can picture things across the senses, shows you where you sit on the aphantasia–hyperphantasia spectrum, and gives you guided exercises to train with — including the mental rehearsal methods used in sport psychology.

Everything stays on your device. The app collects no data, has no accounts, no tracking and no ads.
