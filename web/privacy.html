Mind's Eye Lab — Privacy Policy

Effective [date]. Mind's Eye Lab is a free app from the Imagination and Modal Cognition Lab, Duke University, that measures and trains mental imagery and, with your consent, contributes de-identified data to research.

What we collect

If you consent to research: questionnaire answers, task and game performance (including reaction times and trial-level responses), short text you write in the memory exercises, the demographic answers you choose to give, and technical details (device model, screen size, operating system, locale, timezone, app version). All of it is stored under a participant ID that you create or that a study assigns. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. The optional pupil experiment processes camera frames on your device and stores only pupil-size numbers; no images leave the phone.

If you decline research: everything stays on your device and nothing is sent to us.

How it is used

De-identified data are analysed for research on mental imagery and may be published in aggregate or shared with other researchers in de-identified form. We do not sell data or use it for advertising.

Your choices

You can see all your results in the app at any time. You can stop participating from the Results screen, and choose whether past data are kept or deleted. You can also email your participant ID to [contact email] to have all server data deleted.

Security and retention

Data are transmitted over HTTPS and stored on a server controlled by the research team. Recovery codes are stored hashed. Data are retained for [N] years as required by the study protocol (IRB [number]).

Children

The app is for adults 18 and over.
