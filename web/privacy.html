Mind's Eye — Privacy Policy

Effective 4 October 2026. Mind's Eye is a free, non-commercial app from the Imagination and Modal Cognition Lab at Duke University. It measures and trains mental imagery.

Short version: this app collects nothing. Everything you do stays on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind. We cannot see your results.

What we collect

Nothing. Mind's Eye version 1.0 does not transmit any data off your device. Your questionnaire answers, task and game performance, reaction times, the text you write in the memory exercises, and any demographic details you choose to enter are saved only in the app's local storage on your own device.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, advertising identifier, or device identifiers. The app contains no analytics SDK, no crash reporting service, no advertising network, and no social media integration. It makes no network requests.

How your data is stored

Your results are stored locally by the app on your device. They are included in your device backup if you back your device up, under your own Apple account and subject to Apple's protections — we have no access to that backup.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything the app has stored from the Results screen inside the app. Deleting the app from your device also removes all of its local data.

Children

Mind's Eye is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children — and because the app collects no information at all, none is collected from anyone.

Not a medical device

This app is an educational and self-assessment tool. It is not a medical device, it does not provide medical advice, and it does not diagnose any condition, including aphantasia. Scores describe performance on the app's own tasks and questionnaires only. If you have concerns about your memory, imagery, or cognition, speak to a qualified professional.

Research

This version of the app is not collecting research data. A future version may offer optional participation in mental-imagery research. If that happens, participation will be strictly opt-in, you will be shown a full consent form describing exactly what would be collected and how it would be used before anything is sent, and this policy will be updated and re-dated before that version is released. Using the app will never require taking part in research.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Material changes affecting what is collected will be accompanied by an in-app notice.
