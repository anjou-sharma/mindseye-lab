Mind's Eye — Support

Mind's Eye measures mental imagery across the senses, shows you where you sit on the aphantasia–hyperphantasia spectrum, and gives you exercises and games to train with. It is free, has no ads, and keeps everything on your device.

Getting help

Email anjou.sharma@duke.edu and we will get back to you. Please mention your iPhone model and iOS version if you are reporting a problem.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?
Only on your device. Nothing is uploaded, and we cannot see your results. See the privacy policy.
How do I delete my results?
Open the Results screen and use the erase option. Deleting the app also removes everything it stored.
Will I lose my results if I get a new phone?
Results live on the device. They carry over if you restore the new phone from a backup of the old one; otherwise they start fresh.
Does a low score mean I have aphantasia?
No. Mind's Eye is an educational self-assessment tool, not a diagnostic instrument, and it is not a medical device. Scores describe how you did on these particular tasks. Aphantasia is not an illness, and many people who report little or no visual imagery perform perfectly well on reasoning and memory tasks.
Why do some tasks ask how confident I am?
How well your confidence tracks your accuracy is interesting in its own right, and it can differ from how vivid your imagery feels. The app reports it back to you.
Is this part of a research study?
Not in this version. The app collects no data. A later version may offer optional, opt-in participation in imagery research, which would be fully described before you decided.
The app isn't working properly.
Try closing and reopening it first. If that doesn't help, email us with your iPhone model, your iOS version, and what you were doing when it went wrong.
